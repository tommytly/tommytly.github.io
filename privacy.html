Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Latent is a photo community where you see a photo before you see who took it. It's built and run by one person, Tommy Ly ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: hello@latentphoto.app.

What I collect

  • Account details: your email address, your username, and your display name. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives me an identifier and, if you choose, your name and email (which may be a private relay address).
  • Profile: anything you add yourself — profile photo, bio, and city, state or country.
  • What you post: your photos, captions, and the camera details you keep or type in (camera, lens, settings, film stock). Photos are re-compressed on upload, which removes the original file's metadata. The app does not read GPS location from your photos.
  • What you do on Latent: the impressions and comments you share, discussions (chat messages), who you follow, who you block, and any reports you file.
  • Device and app data: a push notification token (if you allow notifications), crash reports, and basic usage events such as "photo published" or "impression sent".

Photos are also labelled on your device with Apple's Vision framework (for example "portrait" or "landscape") to support future search. The labelling runs on your phone; only the resulting labels are stored.

Why

  • To run the app: show your photos to others, deliver impressions, comments and messages, keep your account secure, and send notifications you've turned on.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, and remove content or accounts that break the Terms.
  • To fix and improve the app: crash reports and usage events tell me what breaks and which features get used.

Latent shows no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or rent your data.

Who can see what

Your photos, captions, username, profile and the impressions you leave are visible to other Latent users. In blind feeds, your name stays hidden on a photo until a viewer shares an impression. Discussions are visible only to the two people in them. Your email address is never shown to other users. Blocks and reports are private.

Service providers

Latent runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions, push delivery, Crashlytics crash reporting and Google Analytics for Firebase), and Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications). They process data on my behalf to provide those services. Data may be stored on servers outside your country.

How long I keep it

I keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, impressions, comments, discussions, follows, blocks and notifications are deleted. Reports you filed, or that were filed about you, are kept so safety decisions can be reviewed. Crash and analytics data is kept by Firebase under its own retention settings.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time in Settings → Delete Account. It's permanent.
  • Edit or remove your profile details and posts in the app.
  • Turn off notifications in Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask any other privacy question, at hello@latentphoto.app.

Children

Latent is not meant for anyone under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child has an account, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, I'll say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
